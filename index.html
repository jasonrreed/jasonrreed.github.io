About

Jason Reed is the Wade Family Associate Teaching Professor of Finance at the University of Notre Dame’s Mendoza College of Business and associate faculty director of the Notre Dame Institute for Global Investing.

Since 2020 he has taught Applied Investment Management, where students run a $32 million live portfolio and report to an advisory board, and Security Analysis, a course in valuing public companies. Outside class he advises the Federal Reserve and Fiscal Policy Challenge Club, whose teams have won six national Fiscal Policy Challenge championships since 2017. His teaching has earned the University’s Rev. Edmund P. Joyce, C.S.C., Award for Excellence in Undergraduate Teaching and the Mendoza College’s Outstanding Undergraduate Professor Award.

An economist by training, he joined the Notre Dame faculty in 2015 after earning his Ph.D. in economics at Wayne State University. From 2019 to 2023 he was assistant chair and director of undergraduate studies in the Department of Finance, where he helped develop and launch a study-abroad program in Dublin. His research brings behavioral economics into macroeconomics and finance, from how adaptive learning shapes exchange rates to behavioral bias among baseball managers.

He has more than 100 media mentions, including a BBC radio interview and coverage in the Financial Times, The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal and CNBC, and is the eighth most-read Notre Dame author on The Conversation. He co-founded and co-hosts two podcasts: All Else Equal, which put students’ questions to Notre Dame faculty, and a new institute podcast of conversations with investors.

He studied art and design alongside economics at Grand Valley State University and still paints in oils. Away from campus he spends time with his family and is an avid traveler.
