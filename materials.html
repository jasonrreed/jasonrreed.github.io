FIN 30210, taught 2015 to 2019

Managerial Economics

The required undergraduate course in the economics of business decisions. It builds from scarcity and opportunity cost through supply and demand, production and cost, and profit maximization to market structure and game theory.

All eleven lessons, with the worked problems solved step by step and the graphs redrawn for the web.

Open the course slides

Managerial Economics lessons

  1. Managerial economics and microeconomicsScarcity, opportunity cost, models and the theory of the firm
  2. Math reviewFunctions, derivatives, partial derivatives and constrained optimization
  3. The essentials of supply and demandDemand, market demand, estimation, supply and equilibrium
  4. ElasticityPrice, income and cross-price elasticity, and elasticity and revenue
  5. ProductionThe production function, Cobb-Douglas, returns to scale and marginal product
  6. Production, part 2Output elasticity, the stages of production and isoquants
  7. CostsShort-run cost curves, learning curves, and economies of scale and scope
  8. Profit and revenue maximizationIsocost lines, optimal inputs, the expansion path and profit
  9. Perfect competition and monopolyShutdown and exit, monopoly pricing, the Lerner index and welfare
  10. Monopolistic competition and oligopolyConcentration, kinked demand, Cournot, Bertrand, Stackelberg and collusion
  11. Introduction to game theoryNash equilibrium, the prisoners’ dilemma, and repeated and sequential games
