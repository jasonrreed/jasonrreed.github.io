Managerial Economics

Lecture slides by Jason R. Reed, PhD
University of Notre Dame

Eleven lessons that build from scarcity and opportunity cost through supply and demand, production and cost, profit maximization, market structure, and game theory.

Lessons

  1. 1 Managerial economics and microeconomics Scarcity, opportunity cost, models, and the theory of the firm
  2. 2 Math review Functions, derivatives, partial derivatives, and constrained optimization
  3. 3 The essentials of supply and demand Demand, market demand, estimation, supply, and equilibrium
  4. 4 Elasticity Price, income, and cross-price elasticity, and elasticity and revenue
  5. 5 Production The production function, Cobb-Douglas, returns to scale, and marginal product
  6. 6 Production, part 2 Output elasticity, the stages of production, and isoquants
  7. 7 Costs Short-run cost curves, learning curves, economies of scale and scope
  8. 8 Profit and revenue maximization Isocost lines, optimal inputs, the expansion path, and profit
  9. 9 Perfect competition and monopoly Shutdown and exit, monopoly pricing, the Lerner index, and welfare
  10. 10 Monopolistic competition and oligopoly Concentration, kinked demand, Cournot, Bertrand, Stackelberg, and collusion
  11. 11 Introduction to game theory Nash equilibrium, the prisoners' dilemma, repeated and sequential games
