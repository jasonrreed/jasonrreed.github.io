Teaching

Jason’s courses teach investing by doing it. Students value real companies, run a real portfolio and argue their calls in front of their peers.

FIN 40640 and MBA 70640, six credits, since 2020

Applied Investment Management

An experiential course in fundamental equity investing for seniors and graduate students, known on campus as AIM. Each section of 25 selected students manages a $32 million live portfolio from Notre Dame’s endowment and reports to an advisory board.

Students research the companies, build the case for each position and settle what the fund owns in investment committee debates. Each year the class takes two investment trips, visiting New York, Boston and Los Angeles. Jason co-teaches AIM with Mark Dumich.

Visit the AIM program

FIN 40610, since 2020

Security Analysis

Intrinsic and relative valuation of U.S. public equities. Each section manages its own live paper portfolio, and lectures run alongside CIO reports, macro discussions and a final project in which students value a company from start to finish.

Building financial models with AI is a core deliverable. In summer 2024 Jason also taught the course at Notre Dame’s London Global Gateway, where 20 finance students learned it in three weeks and met with local investors.

How Jason teaches

His courses are built to move students past remembering material to analyzing, evaluating and creating with it. Students meet new ideas before class through recorded lectures, readings and podcasts, so class time goes to applying them, often mixing practical and theoretical problems.

The other half of the approach is just-in-time teaching. Jason works where his students work, in the trading room or the business library, so questions get answered when they come up. Closing that distance has meant more conversations with students, and longer, more useful ones.
